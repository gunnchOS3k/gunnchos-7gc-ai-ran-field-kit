## Verified public facts (metadata only)

- Rec. ITU-R M.2160 IMT-2030 Framework (RA-23) — APPROVED.
- Usage scenarios (public): IC, HRLLC, MC, UC, AIAC, ISAC.
- Design principles (public portal narrative): security and resilience; connecting the unconnected; ubiquitous intelligence; sustainability.
- WP 5D draft minimum TPRs (Feb 2026) → SG5 approval target Dec 2026 (Document 5/116). Public portal states **20** minimum TPRs; hub article states seven are new/specific to describe 6G performances. Full text TIES-restricted → numeric values = `OFFICIAL_VALUE_PENDING`.
- WP 5D draft evaluation guidelines (June 2026) → SG5 approval target Dec 2026 (Document 5/119). Public portal states three methods (simulation, analytical, inspection) and seven test environments including newly introduced Indoor Factory-HRLLC, Indoor Factory-ISAC, and Urban Macro-ISAC.
- Circular Letter 5/LCCE/115 RIT candidate window 02/2027–02/2029.
- Drafts are **not** a final normative certification basis for product claims.

## Claim boundary

- STANDARDIZED_6G=false, 6G_CERTIFIED=false, GATE_8_PASS=false, CARRIER_ACCEPTED=false.
