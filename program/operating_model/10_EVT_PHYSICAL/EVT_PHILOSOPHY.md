# EVT Philosophy — Risk First

EVT is for questions simulation cannot answer.

EVT0 does not need final cosmetics/tooling/certification. It needs to answer:
- does it power and boot?
- display/touch/storage?
- Wi-Fi/modem/audio?
- Dock?
- Rings communicate?
- gunnchOS/games/AI run?
- what gets hot?
- what fails?

Risk-first order:
1. power/boot
2. compute/storage
3. display/touch
4. I/O/Dock
5. network/modem
6. audio/camera
7. thermal/battery
8. Rings
9. games/local AI
10. Golden Journeys
