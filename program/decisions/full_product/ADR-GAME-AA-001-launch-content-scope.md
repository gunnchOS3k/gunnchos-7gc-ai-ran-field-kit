# ADR-GAME-AA-001 — Anime Aggressors launch content scope

- Status: ACCEPTED (full-product baseline)
- Date: 2026-08-08T00:10:52Z

## Decision
- Launch fighters: **7** (full roster) — data differentiated now; art must leave proxy (`REQUIRES_ART_PRODUCTION`)
- Launch stages: **6** (5 competitive + 1 training) — 3 greybox today is NOT content-complete
- Launch modes minimum: Versus, Training, Arcade ladder, Local multi ruleset variants, Online private lobby + spectator (online may be EXTERNAL for matchmaking infra but client/protocol must exist)
- Competitive CPU: multi-tier, all 7 fighters, large batch eval required before Alpha exit
