# ADR-FP-007 — Student 14.5 display panel freeze

- Status: **ACCEPTED (engineering baseline)**
- Date: 2026-08-08T00:07:41Z

## Decision
- Diagonal: **14.5 in**
- Technology: Oxide IPS LCD (OLED cost-up alternate)
- Resolution: **2880×1800** (or 2560×1600 cost-down) @ 60/120 Hz modes
- Brightness target: ≥400 nits SDR typical; HDR peak TBD measured
- Touch: on-cell capacitive, stylus optional EMR/AES footprint
- Cover: chemically strengthened glass

Exact panel MPN to be locked to a procurable BOE/AUO/Sharp 14.5" eDP part during AVL — until distributor quote, retain dual-footprint in schematic.
