## Decision
- Tier R: scalable import of known taxa (COL/GBIF/PBDB…) — fixtures ≠ global integration
- Tier E: encounter templates — launch floor **≥120** encounter-ready taxa
- Tier F: flagship authored set — **24** flagship taxa/expeditions for launch
- World regions: launch floor **12** (today ~7; polar/biome gaps remain)
- Forbid one-region-as-done and fixture-only as globally integrated
