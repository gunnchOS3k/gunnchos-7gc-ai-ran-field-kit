# Nonphysical Completion Policy

## Purpose

Separate **PASS** (authentic evidence) from **NONPHYSICAL_COMPLETE** (digital totality).

## NONPHYSICAL_COMPLETE

All work classified as AUTOMATABLE_NOW, AUTOMATABLE_AFTER_DEPENDENCY, DIGITAL_DESIGN,
SIMULATION, SOFTWARE, CI, TEST_HARNESS, EVIDENCE_COLLECTOR, FABRICATION_PACKAGE,
DOCUMENTATION, ANALYSIS, EXTERNAL_ENGAGEMENT_PREPARATION, or STANDARDS_MAPPING_CURRENT_STATE
is complete, tested, versioned, and reviewed.

Remaining blockers must be irreducible classes only (hardware, humans, partners, labs,
carriers, manufacturers, purchases, standards finalization, Edmund acceptance).

## Forbidden

- Claiming PASS from nonphysical artifacts
- Labeling simulated/modeled data as MEASURED
- Auto-filling Edmund acceptance
- Fabricating physical evidence
- Asking Edmund to purchase/build during PHYSICAL_EXECUTION_FREEZE unless needed to preserve data

## Allowed claim language

designed for · candidate · prototype architecture · simulation · modeled · development build ·
nonphysical complete · fabrication package complete · ready for physical validation ·
standards mapping current as of `<date>`
